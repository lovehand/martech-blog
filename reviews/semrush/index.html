What is Semrush?

Semrush is a search marketing platform. You use it to find keywords, check your site for technical problems, track rankings, study backlinks and spy on competitors. In the last two years it has also become a tool for tracking how your brand shows up in AI answers from ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity and Gemini.

It is built on a very large dataset. In October 2026 Semrush said its search data covered 29.2 billion keywords, 43 trillion backlinks and 808 million domain profiles, plus more than 2.18 billion AI prompt responses. Its help center lists 142 geographic databases. Those are Semrush's own figures, but they explain why the reports feel so full.

The way Semrush is sold has changed. It used to be simple: pick Pro, Guru or Business. Now the main pricing page sells four plans called SEO, Starter, Pro+ and Advanced. Starter and up bundle the SEO Toolkit with the AI Visibility Toolkit. On top of that you can add specialist toolkits for local, content, social, advertising, PR and traffic data. That makes Semrush more flexible. It also makes the bill harder to predict.

So the real question is not whether Semrush is good. It is. The question is which parts you need.

Key features

Below we cover the tools most buyers care about, what they do well and where the plan limits sit. Limits come from Semrush's official pricing pages and help center, checked November 2026.

Keyword research

Keyword research is where most people start with Semrush, and it is still one of its best tools. Keyword Overview gives you a fast read on any term: search volume, difficulty, intent, trend and the pages that rank. Keyword Magic Tool then expands one seed term into a big list you can filter by volume, difficulty, intent, questions and more.

What we like is how quickly you go from an idea to a short, realistic list. Filters are easy to stack, and Keyword Strategy Builder can turn the list into topic clusters.

There are limits. On the SEO Toolkit tiers Semrush lists 10,000 results per report on Pro, 30,000 on Guru and 50,000 on Business, and 250, 1,000 and 5,000 keyword metric updates a month. Starter, Pro+ and Advanced follow the same limits as Pro, Guru and Business. Historical keyword and domain data, going back to 2012, starts on Pro+.

Also note the difference between the SEO plan and Starter. Semrush describes the $139 SEO plan as having basic keyword research and competitor analysis. Starter is described as having full keyword research and optimization. If keyword research is your main job, Starter is the more sensible entry point.

Site Audit

Site Audit crawls your website and lists technical problems that stop search engines and AI systems from reading it. Each issue is ranked by severity and comes with a short note on how to fix it. A single Site Health score shows progress over time, and thematic reports group issues by area such as crawlability, speed or internal links.

A useful newer check looks at AI crawlers. Semrush says Site Audit checks access for these bots: ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, Googlebot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Claude-User and Claude-SearchBot. If your robots file blocks them by accident, you will see it here.

Crawl limits rise by plan: 100,000 pages a month on Pro, 300,000 on Guru and 1,000,000 on Business, per the SEO Toolkit help page. You can schedule recrawls, get email alerts and send issues straight to Trello. For a small or midsize site this is more than enough. Very large sites with millions of pages are pushed toward Enterprise.

Position Tracking

Position Tracking checks your rankings every day for a list of keywords you choose. You can add up to 20 competitors per campaign, see estimated traffic and visibility, and track SERP features such as featured snippets and AI Overviews.

Daily tracked keywords are one of the clearest limits between plans. The SEO plan and Starter track 500 keywords a day across 5 projects. Pro+ tracks 1,500 across 15 projects. Advanced lists 5,000 or more across 40 or more projects.

Pro+ also unlocks two things agencies and local businesses care about. Multi targeting lets you track the same keywords across several locations, devices or AI platforms inside one project, so you don't burn a project slot for each city. The Keyword Cannibalization report flags pages on your site that compete for the same term. Neither is included on Starter.

Our take: 500 keywords split across 5 sites is about 100 per site. If you manage clients, budget for Pro+.

Backlink analytics

Backlink Analytics shows who links to any domain, how authoritative those links are, the anchor text they use and how the profile has changed over time. Authority Score gives a quick read on domain strength. Backlink Gap shows sites that link to your competitors but not to you, which is the fastest way to build an outreach list. Backlink Audit helps you spot toxic or spammy links.

Semrush's own figure is 43 trillion backlinks. Even Semrush's comparison page calls Ahrefs' backlink index highly regarded. For most marketing teams, Semrush's link data is plenty. A dedicated link building agency should test both.

Competitive research

This is where Semrush really pulls ahead of cheaper tools. Domain Overview shows any site's estimated organic and paid traffic, top keywords, top pages and backlinks in one view. Organic Research breaks down which keywords drive a competitor's traffic. Keyword Gap shows terms your rivals rank for that you don't.

Pro+ adds the Compare Domains report, which benchmarks up to 5 domains, subdomains, folders or URLs side by side. It is ideal for agency pitches.

For deeper market and traffic data you need the separate Traffic & Market Toolkit. Semrush says it covers trends across 190+ countries, up to 100 tracked competitors and history back to 2017. It is priced separately and extra users on it start at $289 a month, so it is a serious add on rather than a casual one.

Content Toolkit

The Content Toolkit is Semrush's AI writing and planning product. It includes Topic Finder for ideas, SEO Brief Generator for outlines, Article Generator for drafts and an optimizer that scores your text against what ranks. There is a Chrome extension, CMS publishing and a brand voice setting.

Semrush sells it as a single plan, Content Base, at $60 a month on its pricing page. The help center says it includes unlimited SEO friendly articles plus 5 SEO boosted pieces a month with deeper SERP analysis, and a 7 day free trial.

Separately, Pro+ and Advanced include content optimization inside the main plan. On the older Guru and Business tiers this was SEO Writing Assistant and Topic Research. If your team already has writers and only needs briefs and optimization, Pro+ may cover you without the Content Toolkit.

AI Visibility and the AI SEO toolkit

This is the biggest change to Semrush in years, and the reason the plans were rebuilt. The AI Visibility Toolkit shows how AI platforms mention your brand and your competitors. It covers Google AI results, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.

There are two main parts. Prompt tracking works like Position Tracking for AI answers. You pick prompts, and Semrush checks each day whether you are cited or mentioned. Brand Performance tracks a wider set of prompts automatically, about 200 according to Semrush, and reports on sentiment and share of voice, updated weekly.

  • SEO plan ($139): includes AI search performance tracking, AI sentiment monitoring and AI visibility reports for any domain, but no dedicated prompt tracking quota and no per domain Brand Performance.
  • Starter: 50 prompts tracked daily, 1 domain for Brand Performance, 300 AI visibility reports a day and an AI readiness check in Site Audit. Also includes MCP access so you can pull Semrush data into AI assistants.
  • Pro+: 100 prompts tracked daily, plus multi targeting so you can track ChatGPT, AI Mode and Google in one project.
  • Advanced: Semrush's pricing page says unlimited prompts tracked daily, while its help center lists 200. We flag that difference. Confirm on the vendor page before you buy.

Two costs to know. Brand Performance covers a single brand on Starter and Pro+, and each extra brand costs $99 a month, according to Semrush. If you only want the AI tools, the AI Visibility Toolkit is also sold on its own, listed by Semrush at $99 a month per domain.

Our take: AI visibility tracking is still young everywhere, and the numbers move a lot day to day. But Semrush puts it next to your normal SEO data, which is where it is most useful. If AI answers matter to your buyers, this is the strongest reason to choose Starter over the cheaper SEO plan. For more tools in this space, see our guide to the best AI marketing tools.

Local SEO

Local SEO lives in the separate Local Toolkit, priced per location. It can be added to any Semrush plan or bought on its own.

  • Local Base, $30 a month per location: GBP AI Agent, which writes and publishes Google Business Profile posts, replies to reviews, posts photos and improves your description. Plus Map Rank Tracker with 375 credits and competitor heatmaps.
  • Local Pro, $60 a month per location: everything in Base, plus listing management across 70+ directories (availability varies by country), review monitoring and generation, AI review replies, GBP insights, bulk management and 1,225 Map Rank Tracker credits.
  • Local Business: a custom offer for businesses with 20+ locations, through sales.

Per location pricing is fair for one shop but scales quickly for chains, so do the math first.

Free plan, trial and refunds

The free plan needs no credit card. You get 1 demo project and 10 reports a day across the main tools, plus some AI visibility features. Enough to test, not enough to run an SEO program.

Paid SEO and AI Search plans, and most specialist toolkits, come with a 7 day free trial. Exports are disabled during trials, which matters if you planned to pull a big keyword list and cancel. You can only trial each toolkit once per account.

Semrush offers a one time 7 day money back guarantee. Per its refund policy, it applies to initial subscriptions with a term of twelve months or more, and to initial add on purchases. It applies to online credit card purchases on Semrush.com. Read the policy before you buy monthly.

Extra users

Every plan includes one user. Semrush's help center says additional users on plans that include the SEO Toolkit start at $45 a month and the price scales with your plan tier. Users on toolkits without the SEO Toolkit, such as Social, Local or Content, cost a flat $20 each. Users on Traffic & Market start at $289 a month. View only access to folders is free.

This is the cost most buyers forget. Ask sales for a quote if you need more than a couple of logins.

Pros

  • The widest feature set in SEO. Keyword research, audits, rank tracking, backlinks, competitor data, content, local and AI visibility in one account.
  • Huge data coverage. Semrush reports 29.2 billion keywords and 43 trillion backlinks across 142 geographic databases.
  • Strong competitor research. Domain Overview, Keyword Gap, Backlink Gap and Compare Domains make it easy to see what rivals do.
  • AI search tracking next to SEO data. Prompt tracking and Brand Performance sit beside your rankings, not in a separate tool.
  • A real free plan and a trial. You can test the core tools before paying.
  • Clear upgrade path. From free to Enterprise without changing tools.
  • Great learning resources. Semrush Academy, a deep help center and a busy blog.

Cons

  • High entry price. $139 a month is a lot for one small site.
  • The add on model adds up. Local, Content, Traffic & Market, reports and extra brands are all extra.
  • Extra users are expensive. From $45 a month each and rising with your plan.
  • Plan names keep changing. Pro, Guru and Business now sit alongside SEO, Starter, Pro+ and Advanced, which confuses buyers.
  • Starter limits are tight for agencies. 5 projects and 500 keywords run out fast.
  • Learning curve. There are so many tools that new users can feel lost for the first few weeks.
  • Trial exports are off, and the money back guarantee only covers annual or longer first subscriptions.

Who Semrush is best for

Semrush is a great fit if you are

  • An in house marketing team that owns SEO, content and competitor research and wants one tool for all of it.
  • An SEO or content agency managing several clients. Pro+ is the plan to look at first.
  • A brand that cares about AI answers. If buyers ask ChatGPT or Google AI about your category, Starter and up give you real tracking.
  • A local business or multi location brand willing to add the Local Toolkit for listings and reviews.
  • A growing company that wants a tool it won't outgrow, with Enterprise options later.

Who should skip Semrush

  • Solo bloggers and tiny sites on a tight budget. The free plan, Google Search Console or a cheaper tool like SE Ranking may be enough.
  • Link builders who only need backlink data. Ahrefs is worth a look.
  • Teams that only need rank tracking. You would be paying for a lot you never open.
  • Companies that mainly need market and traffic intelligence across whole industries. Similarweb is built for that.

Ahrefs

Ahrefs is Semrush's closest rival. Its plans are Lite at $129 a month, Standard at $249 and Advanced at $449. Lite includes 750 tracked keywords, 6 months of history, 150 AI prompt checks, API and MCP access and 1 user. Extra users cost $40 to $80 a month depending on plan. Ahrefs says it never runs discounts and does not offer trials, but Ahrefs Free gives site owners limited access to Site Explorer and Site Audit.

Pick Ahrefs if backlinks and pure SEO research are the heart of your work. Pick Semrush if you want local, content, ads and AI visibility in the same place. Read our Semrush vs Ahrefs comparison for the full breakdown.

Moz Pro

Moz Pro is friendlier for beginners and cheaper to start. Moz lists an entry plan at $49 a month for one website. Standard is $99 a month, or $79 a month paid yearly, with 3 tracked sites and 300 tracked keywords. Medium is $179 a month with 2 users, 10 sites and 1,500 keywords. Large is $299 a month. Moz has added AI visibility dashboards to its plans too.

Pick Moz for a simpler tool with Domain Authority. Pick Semrush for deeper research and higher limits.

SE Ranking

SE Ranking is the value pick. Its Core plan is $129 a month, or $103.20 billed annually, with 10 projects, 2,000 keywords tracked daily, 250,000 audit pages a month, 25 AI prompts a day across 5 AI engines and local and content tools. Growth is $279 a month with 30 projects, 5,000 keywords and 3 seats. There is a 14 day free trial with no card.

Pick SE Ranking if you want generous tracking limits for less money. Pick Semrush if you need the bigger database and more advanced competitor research.

Similarweb

Similarweb is less an SEO tool and more a digital market intelligence platform. It focuses on traffic, audiences, competitor benchmarking, ads and AI traffic trends, with an SEO suite on top. Business plans are custom and go through sales, and there are self service packages for smaller teams. Check the Similarweb pricing page for current options.

Pick Similarweb if your main question is how a whole market or competitor's traffic is moving. Pick Semrush if your main job is ranking and getting cited. More head to head guides are in our comparisons hub.

Frequently asked questions

Is Semrush worth it in 2026?

Yes, if search is a real growth channel for you. Semrush gives you keyword research, technical audits, rank tracking, backlink data, competitor research and AI search tracking in one account. It is expensive for a solo site owner who only needs a few reports, so start with the free plan or the 7 day trial before you pay.

How much does Semrush cost per month?

When we checked in November 2026, Semrush listed the SEO plan at $139 a month, Starter at $199 a month and Pro+ at $299 a month, or $117.33, $165.17 and $248.17 a month billed annually. The Advanced plan and Enterprise are listed on the Semrush pricing page. Add ons such as extra users, Local and Content cost more. Pricing checked November 2026.

What happened to the Semrush Pro, Guru and Business plans?

Semrush's main pricing page now sells SEO, Starter, Pro+ and Advanced plans that bundle SEO with AI visibility. Its help center still lists the SEO Toolkit tiers Pro at $139.95, Guru at $249.95 and Business at $499.95 a month, and says Starter has the same SEO limits as Pro, Pro+ the same as Guru and Advanced the same as Business.

Does Semrush have a free plan or free trial?

Yes to both. The free plan gives you 1 demo project and 10 reports a day across the main tools, with no credit card. Paid SEO and AI Search plans come with a 7 day free trial. Data exports are switched off during the trial.

Is Semrush better than Ahrefs?

Semrush is broader. It covers SEO, AI visibility, local, content, social and ads in one platform. Ahrefs is more focused on SEO and backlink research and has a cheaper entry plan at $129 a month. Pick Semrush if you want one tool for the whole search and content team. Our Semrush vs Ahrefs comparison goes deeper.

Does Semrush track AI search visibility?

Yes. Starter and above include daily prompt tracking across Google AI results, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, plus Brand Performance reports on AI sentiment and share of voice. Starter tracks 50 prompts a day and Pro+ tracks 100. The AI Visibility Toolkit is also sold on its own.

Can I cancel Semrush and get a refund?

You can cancel, upgrade or downgrade at any time unless you signed custom terms. Semrush offers a one time 7 day money back guarantee on initial subscriptions with a term of twelve months or more, and on initial add on purchases. Read the refund policy before buying monthly.

How much does an extra Semrush user cost?

Additional users on plans that include the SEO Toolkit start at $45 a month and the price rises with your plan tier. View only access to folders is free. Check the pricing page for the exact rate on your plan.

Final verdict

Semrush is the most complete SEO platform we have reviewed, and it earns an 8.3 out of 10. The data is deep, the competitor research is excellent, and the new AI visibility tools put it ahead of most rivals for teams that care about AI answers.

The catch is cost and complexity. The entry price is high, add ons make budgeting harder, and extra users are not cheap.

Our advice is simple. Start on the free plan or the 7 day trial. If you need AI tracking, pick Starter over the SEO plan. If you manage several sites or clients, go straight to Pro+. Only add toolkits once you know you will use them every week.
