ActiveCampaign vs HubSpot (2026): Which Should You Buy?

ActiveCampaign is the better pick if email and automation drive your sales and you want serious power for less money. HubSpot is the better pick if you want marketing, sales and service on one free CRM. Here is how they compare on features, real pricing by contact count and team size, ease of use, integrations and support.

Short answer: choose ActiveCampaign if email is how you make money. You get one of the best automation builders on the market from $15 a month, and the plan most teams need costs less than a tenth of HubSpot's Professional tier. Choose HubSpot if your marketing, sales and support teams need to work from the same customer record, and you are happy to pay more for that. We score HubSpot 8.6 and ActiveCampaign 8.5. Quick comparison

These two tools get compared all the time. They overlap a lot. But they come from opposite directions.

ActiveCampaign is an automation tool first. It is built to send the right email to the right person at the right time, without you lifting a finger. A CRM is there if you want it, as a paid add on.

HubSpot is a CRM first. Everything sits on a free shared database, and you add Marketing Hub, Sales Hub or Service Hub on top. Email automation is one part of a much bigger platform.

So the real question is simple. Do you need a great email engine, or a whole customer platform?

Email marketing

This is closer than you might think. Both have a drag and drop editor, templates, A/B testing and personalization. ActiveCampaign includes more than 250 prebuilt templates, dynamic content and scheduled, RSS and date based campaigns on every plan.

The gap opens on the higher plans. ActiveCampaign Pro adds predictive sending, which sends each email at the time each person is most likely to open it. It also adds conditional content, so one email can show different blocks to different people. You get both for $79 a month at 1,000 contacts. HubSpot offers smart content and A/B testing on Marketing Hub Professional, and lists send time optimization as a beta feature.

Sending limits favor ActiveCampaign at the low end. Its Starter plan lets you send 10 times your contact limit each month. HubSpot Starter allows 5 times your marketing contact tier. At the top, HubSpot Enterprise allows 20 times, against 15 times on ActiveCampaign Enterprise. Unless you email your whole list daily, neither limit will bother you.

Automation builder

This is the reason most people buy ActiveCampaign. The builder is a visual canvas. You pick a trigger, then add emails, waits, if and else branches and goals. You can see the whole journey on one screen. There are more than 950 prebuilt recipes to start from, and the AI builder can draft an automation from a plain English request.

The catch is Starter. It caps you at 5 actions per automation, and it is email only. That works for a simple welcome series. It won't hold a real nurture program. Plus removes the cap, adds cross channel steps and sales automation, and costs $49 a month at 1,000 contacts. Pro adds the automations map and A/B testing inside automations.

HubSpot's workflow builder is just as easy to read, and it can act on deals, tickets and companies, not just contacts. That is a big plus if your sales and support teams live in HubSpot too. But the depth you need sits on Marketing Hub Professional. Starter allows up to 10 automated actions in email automation. Professional gives you unlimited actions and up to 300 workflows. Enterprise raises that to 1,000 workflows and adds behavioral events and predictive lead scoring.

Our view: ActiveCampaign gives you HubSpot Professional level automation for the price of a nice lunch. HubSpot gives you automation that reaches across your whole company. Pick the one that matches your problem.

CRM and deals

Here HubSpot is clearly ahead. Its Smart CRM is free and it is the center of everything. Contacts, companies, deals and tickets all share one timeline. HubSpot Starter adds up to 15 pipelines, 500 calling minutes and up to 50 sales workflows. Sales Hub Professional, at $90 per seat per month billed annually, adds sequences, forecasting and playbooks.

ActiveCampaign gives every plan contact profiles, custom fields and tags. That covers the basics. For deals and pipelines you buy the Enhanced CRM, which comes in two versions. Pipelines gives you deals, owners, tasks and deal scoring. Sales Engagement adds automated 1:1 sales emails and win probability. You can only add them on Plus, Pro or Enterprise, and ActiveCampaign does not publish the add on prices on its pricing page. You will see the cost at checkout.

For an agency or a coach who closes deals off the back of email, ActiveCampaign's CRM is fine. For a team of reps on the phone all day, HubSpot is the better home. Our best CRM software guide covers more options if sales is your main need.

Segmentation

ActiveCampaign is very good at deciding who gets what. You can build segments from tags, fields, email engagement, site visits, purchases and custom events. Every plan includes saved segments and AI suggested segments. ActiveCampaign labels segmentation as Limited on Starter, Standard on Plus, Advanced on Pro and Premium on Enterprise. Enterprise adds custom objects for data like subscriptions or bookings.

HubSpot counts segments instead. Starter allows up to 50 active and 1,000 static CRM segments, and the criteria are limited to form data, contact properties and email activity. Professional raises that to 1,200 active and 1,200 static segments. Enterprise allows 2,000 of each. The big HubSpot advantage is that you can segment on deal stage or support tickets, because that data lives in the same CRM.

Landing pages and forms

ActiveCampaign includes forms on every plan, but Starter only gets standard and inline forms. Landing pages and site messages start on Plus. They are simple and do the job.

HubSpot goes much further here. Forms are in the free tools. Landing pages, website pages and blogs live in Content Hub, which is HubSpot's full website builder. The free tools allow up to 30 landing pages with HubSpot branding, and Content Hub Professional allows up to 10,000 website and landing pages. If you want your site, forms and CRM in one system, HubSpot wins easily.

AI features

ActiveCampaign calls its AI Active Intelligence. It can draft campaigns, build automations and segments from a written request, and post weekly insights that flag drops in engagement. Every plan includes 500 AI credits a month, pooled across users. Extra credits come in packs of 100 from $10 a month. Core AI keeps working when credits run out. Only advanced work, such as images and translated campaigns, pauses.

HubSpot's AI is called Breeze. It includes an in app assistant plus AI agents such as Customer Agent, Data Agent and Prospecting Agent. Credits are included by plan: 500 on Starter, 3,000 on Professional and 5,000 on Enterprise. Extra credits cost $0.010 each. In both tools, unused credits do not roll over.

HubSpot's AI does more, because it can act on sales and service data too. ActiveCampaign's AI is simpler and cheaper to run for marketing tasks. Neither is a reason to buy on its own.

Reporting

ActiveCampaign gives every plan campaign, automation and contact reports. Revenue, sales and ecommerce reports start on Plus. Conversion tracking and revenue attribution start on Pro. Custom reporting is an add on for Plus and Pro, and included on Enterprise.

HubSpot gives you 10 dashboards free, 30 on Starter, 75 on Professional and 100 on Enterprise. Custom reports arrive on Professional, with up to 100, and Enterprise allows up to 500. Enterprise also adds multi touch revenue attribution and customer journey analytics. If leadership wants to see which campaigns created which deals, HubSpot does it better, because the deals are already in the system.

Hidden costs to plan for

  • Onboarding. HubSpot charges a required one time fee: $3,000 for Marketing Hub Professional and $7,000 for Enterprise. ActiveCampaign says there are no setup costs, and migration help is free.
  • CRM add ons. ActiveCampaign's Enhanced CRM costs extra and is not priced publicly. HubSpot's CRM is free, but Sales Hub Professional is $90 per seat per month billed annually, plus $1,500 onboarding.
  • Extra seats. ActiveCampaign Plus includes just 1 user. HubSpot Professional extra core seats start at $45 a month.
  • Other add ons. ActiveCampaign sells SMS, custom reporting, transactional email and WhatsApp separately.
  • AI credits. ActiveCampaign packs of 100 start at $10 a month. HubSpot credits cost $0.010 each. Translated campaigns on ActiveCampaign use about 0.04 credits per contact, so big sends add up.
  • Promotions. HubSpot's $7 Starter price is for new customers for a limited time. Budget for $20 at renewal unless your contract says otherwise.

Ease of use

Both tools are easier than most enterprise software. They are easy in different ways.

ActiveCampaign: easy builder, busy app

The automation builder is the best part of the product to use. It reads like a flowchart. You can see who enters, where they go and why. The recipe library means you rarely start from a blank canvas. And the AI builder can give you a working first draft in seconds.

The wider app is busier. There are a lot of menus, settings and add ons. New users can take a while to find where lists, tags and fields live. Our ActiveCampaign review scores ease of use at 8.2 for that reason.

The real risk is mess. A busy account can end up with dozens of automations that trigger each other. Name things clearly from day one. If you are on Pro, use the automations map to see how they connect.

HubSpot: clean, consistent and guided

HubSpot is famous for being easy, and it mostly earns it. Every record looks the same. Settings are explained in plain words. A founder can import a list, build a form and send a first email in an afternoon. Our HubSpot review scores ease of use at 8.8.

HubSpot's catch is size. The platform is now huge, and the pricing model mixes seats, contacts, Hubs and credits. Using HubSpot is easy. Working out what you need to buy is not.

Our take: if your team is one marketer building email flows, ActiveCampaign will feel natural fast. If several teams need to share the tool, HubSpot is easier to roll out.

Support

Support depends on the plan you buy, so check this before you sign.

ActiveCampaign support

  • Every plan: email and chat support, community access, ActiveTraining, video tutorials, workshops and a knowledge base.
  • Starter and Plus: basic support and basic onboarding and migration help.
  • Pro and Enterprise: priority support and priority onboarding.
  • Enterprise: a dedicated account team and an uptime SLA.

ActiveCampaign also offers a full refund if you are not satisfied after 30 days. That takes a lot of risk out of trying it.

HubSpot support

  • Free: HubSpot Community, HubSpot Academy and the knowledge base.
  • Starter: adds email support.
  • Professional and Enterprise: email and phone support.
  • Languages: support in several languages, including English, French, German, Japanese and Portuguese.

HubSpot Academy is a real strength. The courses are free and teach the marketing skill, not just the buttons.

Our take: HubSpot has the better learning resources. ActiveCampaign has the better deal on lower plans, with free migration and chat support from day one.

Which one should you choose?

Here is how we would decide in the most common situations.

You run an ecommerce store

Choose ActiveCampaign. Abandoned cart, post purchase and win back flows are what it does best. Shopify and WooCommerce connect on every plan. Plus adds product blocks and coupons. You get all of that for far less than HubSpot Professional.

You are a creator, coach or course business

Choose ActiveCampaign. Your business runs on email sequences, tags and branching logic. That is ActiveCampaign's home ground. Start on Plus so you are not stuck with the 5 action limit.

You are a B2B company with a sales team

Choose HubSpot. When reps need to see which emails a lead opened, and marketing needs to report on closed deals, one shared CRM saves real pain. Start on Starter. Move to Professional once you will actually use workflows, lead scoring and custom reports.

You are a solo marketer or a team of 2 or 3

It depends on what you need first. If you mainly send newsletters and want a free CRM, HubSpot's free tools and Starter plan are hard to beat. If you need branching automations now, ActiveCampaign Pro gives a 3 person team serious power for $79 a month at 1,000 contacts.

You are an agency

Either can work. ActiveCampaign is cheaper per client and great for automation builds. HubSpot has a big partner program and makes sense when clients want a website, CRM and marketing in one place.

Your team already uses Salesforce or Pipedrive

Lean ActiveCampaign. Pair it with Pipedrive on any plan. With Salesforce, budget for Enterprise. If you are weighing CRMs at the same time, read our HubSpot vs Salesforce comparison.

Marketing, sales and support all need the same data

Choose HubSpot. This is the one job ActiveCampaign is not built for. HubSpot's shared record is the reason to pay more.

Frequently asked questions

Is ActiveCampaign cheaper than HubSpot?

For email marketing and automation, yes, by a wide margin. At 1,000 contacts billed yearly, ActiveCampaign Pro costs $79 a month with 3 users and no onboarding fee. HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional costs $800 a month with 3 core seats and 2,000 marketing contacts, plus a required $3,000 one time onboarding fee. At the entry level the gap is small: ActiveCampaign Starter is from $15 a month and HubSpot Starter is $20 per seat per month at the standard price.

Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan like HubSpot?

No. ActiveCampaign offers a 14 day free trial with Professional plan features, 1 user and up to 100 email sends. HubSpot has free tools with no time limit for up to 2 users, with limits such as 1,000 contacts and 2,000 marketing email sends a month.

Is ActiveCampaign a real CRM?

Partly. Every ActiveCampaign plan has contact profiles, custom fields and tags. Pipelines, deal records and lead scoring come from the Enhanced CRM add ons, which you can only buy on Plus, Pro or Enterprise. HubSpot includes its Smart CRM free and sells Sales Hub for deeper sales tools.

Which has the better automation builder?

Both are visual and easy to read. ActiveCampaign gives you unlimited automation actions from the Plus plan at $49 a month, plus 950+ prebuilt recipes. HubSpot limits Starter to 10 automated actions and opens up unlimited actions and omnichannel workflows on Marketing Hub Professional. For depth per dollar, ActiveCampaign wins.

Can I use ActiveCampaign with HubSpot or Salesforce?

ActiveCampaign connects to more than 1,000 apps. Standard CRM integrations such as Pipedrive, Zoho and Freshsales work on every plan. Premium CRM integrations for Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics are Enterprise only. If you run Salesforce for sales, budget for ActiveCampaign Enterprise to get the native sync.

Do ActiveCampaign and HubSpot include AI?

Yes. Every ActiveCampaign plan includes 500 Active Intelligence credits a month, with extra packs of 100 from $10 a month. HubSpot includes 500 HubSpot Credits on Starter, 3,000 on Professional and 5,000 on Enterprise, with extra credits at $0.010 each. Neither vendor rolls unused credits over.

How do the two charge as my list grows?

Both charge by contacts. ActiveCampaign prices each plan in contact bands that all start at 1,000 contacts. HubSpot Marketing Hub charges for marketing contacts, with 1,000 included on Starter, 2,000 on Professional and 10,000 on Enterprise. HubSpot lets you store non marketing contacts for free, but you cannot downgrade a contact tier until renewal.

Which is better for a small business?

If you sell mainly through email, ActiveCampaign is the better value. If you want one free CRM that marketing, sales and service all share, start with HubSpot's free tools and Starter plan. Many small teams try both, since HubSpot is free and ActiveCampaign has a 14 day trial and a 30 day money back guarantee.

Final recommendation

For email led businesses, ActiveCampaign is the better choice. The automation builder is excellent, segmentation is strong, there is no onboarding fee, and Pro costs $79 a month at 1,000 contacts. Start the 14 day trial, build one real automation, and pick Plus or Pro based on your team size.

For teams that need marketing, sales and service to share one customer record, HubSpot is the better choice. Start with the free tools, move to Starter, and only step up to Professional when you will use the workflows and reporting. Budget for the onboarding fee in year one.

Still torn? Try both. HubSpot's free tools have no time limit. ActiveCampaign offers a 14 day trial and a 30 day money back guarantee. Import a real list, build your welcome series in each, and see which one your team wants to open on Monday.
