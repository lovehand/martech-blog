The quick answer

The best email marketing software for most businesses in 2026 is ActiveCampaign. Its automation builder is one of the best you can buy, and plans start at $15 a month for 1,000 contacts on annual billing. If your sales team needs to see every email a lead opened, choose HubSpot Marketing Hub. If you run an online store, choose Klaviyo. 1. ActiveCampaign: best email marketing software for most businesses

2. HubSpot Marketing Hub: best for email plus CRM on one platform

Our score: 8.6 / 10. HubSpot is the strongest all in one choice here. Your emails, forms, contacts, deals and support tickets live on the same record. You pay for that convenience, especially once you hit Professional.

Overview

HubSpot prices Marketing Hub on marketing contacts, meaning the contacts you can actually email or target with ads. You can store other contacts as non marketing contacts for free. That design rewards teams who keep their lists clean.

Email marketing software buying guide

Most email software regret comes from three mistakes. Picking on the free plan alone. Underestimating how fast the bill grows with your list. Or buying automation depth you never set up. Here is how to avoid all three.

How to choose email marketing software

  1. Decide what email is for. A monthly newsletter needs very different tools from abandoned cart flows or a B2B lead nurture.
  2. Count your contacts for year two. Price the plan at about one and a half times your current list so growth doesn't surprise you.
  3. Check how the tool charges. Contacts, active profiles, marketing contacts, seats or emails sent. Each model favors a different kind of sender.
  4. List your must have integrations. Your store, CRM, forms, website and ads. Check each one is native on the plan you can afford.
  5. Build one real automation in the trial. A welcome series with a branch is enough to see how the builder feels.
  6. Get the full price in writing. Plan, contact tier, seats, add-ons, AI credits and onboarding fees.

Features that matter

Every vendor lists hundreds of features. These are the ones that change results.

  • Automation with branching. If a tool can't send a different email to people who clicked and people who didn't, it will hold you back.
  • Segmentation. Filter by behavior, purchases and custom fields, not just tags.
  • Deliverability basics. Custom sending domain, SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup help, and tools to clean your list.
  • Forms and landing pages. You need a way to grow the list, ideally without another tool.
  • Reporting that ties to revenue. Opens are noisy. Clicks, conversions and revenue tell you what works.
  • Testing. A/B testing of subject lines and content, plus send time optimization on higher plans.
  • AI that saves time. Drafting, segment building and send time help are useful. Watch for usage based credits.

Contacts vs emails sent: which pricing model wins?

Most tools here charge by list size. ActiveCampaign and Mailchimp count contacts. Klaviyo counts active profiles. MailerLite counts active subscribers. HubSpot counts marketing contacts. Brevo is the odd one out, charging mainly by emails sent.

Here is the practical difference. A store with 40,000 contacts that emails once a month pays for 40,000 contacts on a contact based tool. On Brevo it pays for the sends. A creator with 2,000 subscribers who emails three times a week sends a lot relative to list size, so a contact based plan with generous send limits, or MailerLite Power with no sending cap, is easier to predict.

List size scenarios

Just starting, under 1,000 contacts. Start free or cheap. Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers if you mostly broadcast. MailerLite Comfort costs $12 a month at 500 subscribers if you want automation and a clean editor. ActiveCampaign Starter at $15 a month for 1,000 contacts billed yearly is the pick if you know automation will drive sales.

Growing, 1,000 to 10,000 contacts. This is where the choice matters most. Choose ActiveCampaign Plus or Pro for serious automation. Choose Klaviyo if you run a store. Choose MailerLite if you want simplicity at a lower price. Its published rates run from $19 at 1,000 subscribers to $89 at 10,000 on Comfort, paid monthly.

Scaling, 10,000 to 100,000 contacts. Compare total cost carefully. Brevo can be much cheaper if you send rarely. ActiveCampaign Pro and Enterprise and Klaviyo scale well for active senders. HubSpot Professional makes sense when sales needs the same record.

Large or regulated teams. Look at ActiveCampaign Enterprise for SSO and HIPAA support, HubSpot Enterprise for attribution across marketing and sales, Klaviyo Custom above 150,000 profiles, or Brevo Enterprise above a million contacts. Pair your choice with a solid analytics setup. Our best analytics and attribution tools guide covers that side.

What about deliverability?

Every vendor here says it takes deliverability seriously, and the big ones manage shared sending IPs for most customers. Dedicated IPs are offered on some higher plans or as add-ons. Brevo includes one on Enterprise and MailerLite sells one as an add-on. In practice your own habits matter more than the platform. Authenticate your domain, send to people who asked to hear from you, and remove contacts who never open. We haven't run our own inbox placement tests for this guide, so we don't rank tools on deliverability numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best email marketing software in 2026?

ActiveCampaign is the best email marketing software for most small and midsize businesses because it pairs a powerful visual automation builder with a low entry price of $15 a month for 1,000 contacts on annual billing. HubSpot Marketing Hub is the better choice if you want email, CRM and sales on one record. Klaviyo is the best pick for ecommerce brands, and MailerLite is the best value for beginners.

What is the best free email marketing software?

It depends on what you need. Kit is free for up to 10,000 subscribers, and Kit says there are no sending limits, which makes it the most generous free plan we found for a list you actually email. Brevo lets you store up to 100,000 contacts free but caps sending at 300 emails a day. MailerLite's free plan covers 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails a month, and Mailchimp's covers 250 contacts and 500 sends a month. HubSpot's free tools include 2,000 email sends a month with HubSpot branding.

How much does email marketing software cost?

Entry plans for a small list usually cost between about $9 and $25 a month. ActiveCampaign Starter is $15 a month for 1,000 contacts billed yearly, MailerLite Comfort starts at $12 a month for 500 subscribers, Brevo Starter is $9 a month and Klaviyo's Email plan is $20 a month for 500 active profiles. Prices rise as your list grows, and all in one platforms like HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional cost $800 a month plus onboarding.

Is ActiveCampaign better than Mailchimp?

For automation, yes. ActiveCampaign has deeper branching automation, stronger segmentation and more than 950 prebuilt automation recipes. Mailchimp is easier for a first newsletter and has a free plan, which ActiveCampaign does not. If you mainly send a monthly newsletter, Mailchimp or MailerLite is enough. If email nurture drives your sales, ActiveCampaign is worth the move.

Is HubSpot good for email marketing?

Yes, especially if your sales team also works in HubSpot. Marketing Hub shares one record with the Smart CRM, so reps see every email a lead opened. The trade off is price. Marketing Hub Professional costs $800 a month with 3 core seats and 2,000 marketing contacts, plus a $3,000 one time onboarding fee.

Which email marketing platform is best for ecommerce?

Klaviyo is the best fit for most online stores because store data, segments, flows and predictions all sit on the same customer profile. ActiveCampaign is a strong lower cost alternative, with abandoned cart automation and Shopify and WooCommerce integrations on every plan.

Should I choose a platform priced by contacts or by emails sent?

If you have a big list and send rarely, pricing by emails sent, like Brevo, is usually cheaper. If you have a smaller, engaged list and send often, contact based pricing like ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp or MailerLite is easier to predict. Either way, clean inactive contacts regularly so you don't pay for people who never open.

Can I switch email marketing software later?

Yes. Every tool on this list lets you import contacts by CSV, and ActiveCampaign offers free migration help. The hard part is rebuilding automations, templates and forms, so pick a platform that fits where your list and team will be in two years.
