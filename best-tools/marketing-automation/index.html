Best Marketing Automation Software (2026)

HubSpot Marketing Hub is the best marketing automation software for most growing businesses. ActiveCampaign is the best value for email led teams. Klaviyo wins for ecommerce, and Brevo is the pick on a tight budget. Here is how all six compare on price, features and fit.

The quick answer

The best marketing automation software for most businesses in 2026 is HubSpot Marketing Hub. Your marketing, sales and service teams all work from one CRM record, and the workflow builder is easy to run without a full time admin. If email is how you make money and you want deep automation for less, choose ActiveCampaign. If you sell on Shopify, choose Klaviyo. If budget comes first, start with Brevo.

Bigger companies have two more serious options. Adobe Marketo Engage is built for B2B demand generation with complex scoring and account based marketing. Salesforce Marketing Cloud makes sense when your company already runs on Salesforce and wants marketing on the same platform.

That is the short version. The rest of this guide shows what each tool really costs, where each one shines, and the traps that make a cheap plan expensive fast.

1. HubSpot Marketing Hub: best marketing automation for most businesses

Our score: 8.6 / 10. HubSpot is the platform we recommend most often. Not because it is cheap. Because it puts every customer touchpoint on one record, and the automation is easy enough for a small team to run well.

Overview

Marketing Hub sits on top of HubSpot's Smart CRM. Your emails, forms, landing pages, ads, social posts and workflows all write to the same contact record your sales team uses. A rep can see which emails a lead opened. A marketer can build a list from deal stage. Nothing has to be synced.

The free tools and Starter plan cover the basics. Professional is where Marketing Hub turns into a real automation platform, with omnichannel workflows, lead scoring and custom reporting. Breeze, HubSpot's AI layer, adds agents that run on HubSpot Credits.

Key features

  • Workflows. The free tools allow 1 automated email action and Starter up to 10. Professional unlocks up to 300 omnichannel workflows, and Enterprise up to 1,000 with behavioral events and predictive lead scoring.
  • Email sends. 2,000 a month with branding on free tools. Starter allows 5 times your marketing contact tier each month, Professional 10 times and Enterprise 20 times.
  • Lead scoring. Up to 5 scores on Professional and up to 50 on Enterprise, with AI recommendations.
  • Reporting. Dashboards rise from 10 on free tools to 30, 75 and 100. Enterprise adds multi touch revenue attribution and customer journey analytics.
  • AI agents. Customer Agent, Data Agent, Prospecting Agent and more, some in beta. Starter includes 500 credits a month, Professional 3,000 and Enterprise 5,000.
  • AEO tracking. HubSpot AEO shows how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, listed at $45 a month billed annually for 25 prompts.

2. ActiveCampaign: best value for email led automation

Our score: 8.5 / 10. ActiveCampaign gives you one of the most capable automation builders on the market for a fraction of HubSpot Professional. If email drives your revenue, this is the one to beat.

Overview

ActiveCampaign is marketing first and CRM second. Its core job is sending the right email to the right person at the right time, automatically. The visual builder lets you set a trigger, then add waits, conditions, goals and branches. You can see a whole journey on one screen.

Every plan includes more than 950 prebuilt automation recipes. Active Intelligence, its AI layer, can draft campaigns, automations and segments from a plain English request. The sales CRM, called Enhanced CRM, is a paid add-on from the Plus plan.

Key features

  • Automation builder. Multistep automations on every plan. Starter caps you at 5 actions per automation. Plus removes the cap and adds cross channel steps.
  • Segmentation. Build segments from tags, fields, engagement, site visits, purchases and custom events. Depth rises by plan, and Enterprise adds custom objects.
  • Predictive sending and conditional content. On Pro and Enterprise.
  • AI credits. 500 a month on every plan. Extra packs of 100 start at $10 a month.
  • Integrations. 1,000+ integrations, Zapier, API and webhooks, plus MCP access from Claude and ChatGPT. Native Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics sync needs Enterprise.
  • Ecommerce. Shopify, WooCommerce and Square on every plan, with abandoned cart flows built in.

3. Klaviyo: best for ecommerce brands

Our score: 8.4 / 10. Klaviyo is built around store data. Orders, products, browsing and predicted lifetime value all feed your flows. For a Shopify brand, that makes it the natural first choice.

Overview

Klaviyo calls itself a B2C CRM. In plain English, it pulls your store's customer and order data into one profile, then uses it to trigger email, SMS and push messages. A browse abandonment flow, a post purchase thank you and a win back series can all key off real purchase behavior, not just tags.

The Shopify link is the big draw. Klaviyo says more than 117,000 brands use its native Shopify integration. Klaviyo has also announced Klaviyo in Shopify, a free lightweight version inside the Shopify admin for new stores with fewer than 250 contacts.

Key features

  • Campaigns and flows. Included on every plan, including free. Flows can start when someone joins a list or segment, or when they take an action such as placing an order.
  • Segmentation. Segment by browsing behavior, purchase history, channel preference, custom properties and predicted customer lifetime value.
  • A/B testing. On paid plans, not on the free plan.
  • SMS and mobile. SMS and MMS are an add-on on paid plans. Mobile app push is included.
  • AI and service. Composer, a Customer Agent and Helpdesk come with small free allowances and are add-ons beyond that.
  • Custom plan. Over 150,000 active profiles you move to a custom plan with volume pricing, customizable attribution and data warehouse sync.

4. Brevo: best budget marketing automation

Our score: 8.3 / 10. Brevo gives you unlimited workflows, A/B testing and send time optimization for $18 a month. Nothing else here comes close on price for that feature set.

Overview

Brevo used to be called Sendinblue. Brevo says more than 600,000 customers use it. The pricing model is the key difference. Brevo charges mainly by email volume, not by how many contacts you store. That makes it a great fit for businesses with big lists they email now and then.

It also bundles more channels than you might expect. Transactional email, SMS credits, WhatsApp on higher plans and a basic sales CRM are all part of the platform.

Key features

  • Free plan. Brevo's help center says the Free plan includes 300 email sends a day, storage for up to 100,000 contacts and automations that up to 2,000 unique contacts can enter.
  • Marketing automation. Standard is the first plan Brevo lists with unlimited multistep workflows, plus web and event tracking to trigger them.
  • Testing and timing. A/B testing and AI send time optimization from Standard.
  • Segmentation. Advanced segmentation from Starter, with filters for website activity, engagement and custom events.
  • More channels. WhatsApp, popups, and mobile and web push on Professional.
  • Scoring. Contact scoring, including RFM and CLV, on Professional.

Marketing automation buying guide

Most marketing automation regret comes from three mistakes. Buying a platform built for a different business model. Underestimating how pricing grows with your list. Or buying power your team doesn't have time to use. Here is how to avoid all three.

How to choose marketing automation software

  1. Name your business model. B2B lead nurture, ecommerce, or product led SaaS. Each one points to a different shortlist. HubSpot and Marketo lean B2B. Klaviyo leans ecommerce. Customer.io leans product led.
  2. Map three workflows you need first. For example a welcome series, a lead nurture and an abandoned cart flow. If a tool can't build those simply, cross it off.
  3. Decide where your CRM lives. If sales lives in Salesforce, check the native sync and which plan it needs. ActiveCampaign, for example, keeps its Salesforce integration on Enterprise.
  4. Price it at year two. Take your list size, multiply by one and a half, and price the plan you will need at that number.
  5. Check who will run it. Brevo and ActiveCampaign can be run by a generalist. Marketo and Marketing Cloud usually need a specialist.
  6. Run a real trial. Import real contacts, build one real workflow and send it to a test segment. See how it feels after a week, not an hour.

Features that matter

  • A readable workflow builder. You will revisit automations months later. If you can't read them at a glance, they will break quietly.
  • Behavioral triggers. Website visits, purchases, form fills and custom events. This is what separates automation from scheduled email.
  • Segmentation. Saved, dynamic segments that update on their own as data changes.
  • Lead scoring. Essential for B2B. It tells sales who to call first.
  • CRM sync. Two way, near real time, and on the plan you can afford.
  • Attribution and reporting. You need to see which programs create revenue, not just opens. Our best analytics and attribution tools guide covers this in more depth.
  • Deliverability tools. Domain authentication, list hygiene and honest open rate reporting.

Team size scenarios

Solo founder or creator. Start free or nearly free. Brevo Free or Standard gives you real workflows on a tiny budget. ActiveCampaign Starter at $15 a month is a step up in automation depth. Klaviyo's free plan works if you run a small store.

Small team of 2 to 10. HubSpot Starter if sales and marketing need to share data. ActiveCampaign Plus or Pro if email nurture is the engine. Klaviyo Email or Marketing if you sell online.

Growing team of 10 to 50. This is HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional territory, or ActiveCampaign Pro and Enterprise if you want to keep costs down. Ecommerce brands at scale will stay on Klaviyo and watch the profile count.

Enterprise or complex B2B. Marketo Engage for sophisticated demand generation. Salesforce Marketing Cloud if you are already a Salesforce company. HubSpot Enterprise is a credible, easier alternative. Our best CRM software guide helps if the CRM decision is still open.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best marketing automation software in 2026?

HubSpot Marketing Hub is the best marketing automation software for most small and midsize businesses because marketing, sales and service share one CRM record and the workflows are easy to run without an admin. ActiveCampaign is the best value pick for email led businesses, Klaviyo is the best choice for ecommerce brands, and Brevo is the best budget option.

What is the best free marketing automation software?

Brevo has the most useful free plan for automation on this list. Brevo says its Free plan includes 300 email sends per day, storage for up to 100,000 contacts and automations that up to 2,000 unique contacts can enter. HubSpot's free tools are free for up to 2 users but allow just one automated email action. Klaviyo's free plan covers 250 active profiles and 500 emails a month.

How much does marketing automation software cost?

Entry plans start low. Brevo Starter is $9 a month, ActiveCampaign Starter is from $15 a month for 1,000 contacts billed yearly, and Klaviyo's Email plan is $20 a month for 500 active profiles. Mid tier platforms cost more, with HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional at $800 a month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee. Enterprise tools such as Salesforce Marketing Cloud Next Growth start at $1,500 per org per month billed annually, and Adobe Marketo Engage is quote based. Pricing checked November 2026.

Is HubSpot or ActiveCampaign better for marketing automation?

ActiveCampaign is better if email and automation drive your revenue and you want deep workflows at a low price. HubSpot is better if you want marketing, sales and service on one platform with a free CRM to start. Our ActiveCampaign vs HubSpot comparison covers the trade offs in detail.

What is the difference between email marketing and marketing automation?

Email marketing is sending campaigns to a list. Marketing automation is the system that decides who gets which message and when, based on behavior, data and rules. Most modern tools do both, but the depth of branching, scoring, segmentation and cross channel steps is what separates real automation platforms from newsletter tools.

Do small businesses need marketing automation?

If you collect leads or customers online and follow up by email, yes. A simple welcome series, an abandoned cart flow and a re-engagement sequence can run every day without anyone touching them. Start small on a free or low cost plan and add complexity only when a workflow proves its value.

Is Salesforce Marketing Cloud worth it for small businesses?

Usually not. Salesforce lists Marketing Cloud Next Growth Edition at $1,500 per org per month billed annually, on top of your CRM seats. Small businesses already on Salesforce can start with Salesforce Starter, which includes email marketing from $25 per user per month. Most small teams get more value from HubSpot, ActiveCampaign or Brevo.

Can I switch marketing automation platforms later?

Yes, but plan for it. Contacts and lists export easily as CSV files. Workflows, templates, scoring models and reporting history usually do not, so you rebuild them in the new tool. Several vendors offer migration help, and ActiveCampaign advertises free migration. Choose a tool you can grow into for two to three years.
