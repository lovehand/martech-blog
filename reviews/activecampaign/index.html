What is ActiveCampaign?

ActiveCampaign is a marketing automation platform. Its core job is sending the right email to the right person at the right moment, without you doing it by hand. It started as a small business tool in 2003 and the company says it now serves more than 150,000 customers.

Think of it in three layers. At the bottom is your contact database, with tags, custom fields and behavior tracking from your website and store. In the middle sit email campaigns and the automation builder, which is the product's real strength. On top are optional extras: the Enhanced CRM for sales pipelines, SMS, WhatsApp, custom reporting and transactional email.

Across all of it runs Active Intelligence, ActiveCampaign's AI layer. It can draft campaigns, build automations and segments from a plain English request, and flag problems in your account before you notice them.

The key thing to understand before buying: ActiveCampaign is marketing first and CRM second. It is not trying to be Salesforce. If your business sells mostly through email, that focus is a strength. If you have a sales team making calls all day, you will probably pair it with a dedicated CRM. Our best marketing automation tools guide shows where it fits.

Key features

Below we cover each major part of the platform, what it does well and where the limits sit by plan. Plan limits come from ActiveCampaign's pricing page and Help Center, checked November 2026.

Email marketing

Email is the heart of ActiveCampaign, and it shows. Every plan includes a drag and drop designer for HTML and plain text emails, more than 250 prebuilt templates, scheduled and RSS triggered campaigns, date based campaigns, autoresponders, dynamic content and A/B testing.

Sending limits are tied to your contact count. Starter and Plus allow monthly sends of 10 times your contact limit. Pro allows 12 times and Enterprise 15 times. So at 1,000 contacts you get 10,000, 12,000 or 15,000 sends a month depending on the plan. For most businesses that is plenty. If you email your whole list every day, do the math first.

The higher plans add the features that actually lift results:

  • Conditional content (Pro and Enterprise) lets you build one email that shows different blocks to different contacts.
  • Predictive sending (Pro and Enterprise) sends each email at the time each contact is most likely to engage.
  • Inbox preview and testing shows how emails render across clients. Plus and Pro get 5 previews a month. Enterprise gets unlimited. Starter does not include it.
  • AI content and image generation is included on Plus and above, not on Starter.

Our take: the editor is solid rather than spectacular. Where ActiveCampaign pulls ahead is what happens after the email goes out.

Automation builder

This is why people buy ActiveCampaign. The automation builder is a visual canvas where you set a trigger, then add actions, waits, conditions and branches. A contact joins, gets an email, waits two days, and then goes down one path if they clicked and another if they didn't. You can see the whole journey on one screen.

Every plan includes multistep automation, multiple triggers, ecommerce automation and more than 950 prebuilt automation recipes you can import and edit. You can also describe what you want in plain English and let the AI powered automation builder create a first draft.

The plan differences matter here:

  • Starter is limited to 5 actions per automation and email only. That is enough for a welcome series or simple abandoned cart flow, but you will feel the ceiling quickly.
  • Plus removes the action limit and adds cross channel steps, sales automation and site messages.
  • Pro adds the automations map (a bird's eye view of how your automations connect), A/B testing inside automations and an advanced set of triggers and actions.
  • Enterprise adds a premium set of triggers and actions on top.

In our evaluation the builder scores highly on both features and ease of use. Branching logic is easy to read, goals let contacts skip ahead when they convert, and the recipe library saves real setup time. Compared with HubSpot's workflows, ActiveCampaign gives you this depth on a far cheaper plan. The main risk is complexity creep. Without naming rules and the automations map, a busy account turns into spaghetti fast.

CRM and deals

Every plan includes contact profiles, custom fields and tags, so you always have a basic CRM view of each person. Starter limits you to 5 lists.

The sales CRM, with pipelines, deal records, tasks and forecasting, is a separate purchase called the Enhanced CRM. It comes in two versions, and you can only buy them on Plus, Pro or Enterprise:

  • Pipelines gives you deals, pipelines, deal owners, tasks, deal scoring, deal based automation triggers and sales reports such as funnel, sales performance and deal forecast.
  • Sales Engagement includes all of that plus automated 1:1 sales emails, win probability scoring and sentiment change triggers.

ActiveCampaign's Help Center says you can create an unlimited number of pipelines, and you can send direct 1:1 emails from a deal record through your connected inbox. ActiveCampaign does not list Enhanced CRM add on prices on its public pricing page, so you will see the cost at checkout or from sales.

Here is our honest view. The Enhanced CRM is good enough for a small team that sells off the back of marketing, such as an agency or a coaching business. It is not a replacement for a dedicated sales CRM if you have a team of reps working phones and pipelines all day. For that, look at Pipedrive or Salesforce, both of which ActiveCampaign connects to.

Segmentation

Segmentation decides who gets what, and ActiveCampaign does it well. You can build segments from tags, custom fields, email engagement, site visits, purchase history and custom events, then save them for reuse.

Depth depends on the plan. ActiveCampaign labels segmentation as Limited on Starter, Standard on Plus, Advanced on Pro and Premium on Enterprise. Every plan gets saved segments and AI suggested segments. Enterprise adds custom objects, which let you store data that doesn't fit neatly on a contact, such as subscriptions, bookings or vehicles.

There is also a Segments agent, available in beta through Active Intelligence, that builds and edits segments from a written description. It is a real time saver for teams without a marketing ops person.

AI features: Active Intelligence

Active Intelligence is ActiveCampaign's name for its AI layer. It has grown quickly, and ActiveCampaign now calls its latest release Active Intelligence 3.0, nicknamed Wavelength. Here is what it does in practice:

  • Campaign builder. Describe a campaign and it drafts the email, subject line and audience.
  • Automation builder. Describe a workflow and it builds a first version you can edit.
  • Segments agent. Builds audience segments from plain English (beta).
  • Autonomous Insights. Weekly cards on your homepage that flag engagement drops and suggest fixes. Available to all paid customers.
  • Brand kit. Stores your brand look and tone. Starter gets 1 brand kit, other plans get 5.
  • Sidebar. An AI helper that follows you around the app. Access is limited on Starter and Plus and unlimited on Pro and Enterprise.

Some AI actions use AI credits. Every plan includes 500 a month. Credits are pooled across users, reset monthly and don't roll over. Extra credits come in packs of 100, starting at $10 a month. Core actions, like creating a campaign or automation in basic form, keep working even when credits run out. Advanced work, such as generating images, sending translated campaigns and running AI actions inside automations, pauses until credits reset or you buy more.

ActiveCampaign's own estimates give a feel for usage. An image operation uses about 3 credits. A translated campaign or an AI action in an automation uses about 0.04 credits per contact. So a translated send to 10,000 contacts would use roughly 400 credits, most of your monthly allowance. If you plan to lean on AI translations or AI steps inside big automations, budget for credit packs.

Our take: the AI is useful for drafting and spotting problems, and the credit model is simple. Watch per contact actions, because they scale with your list.

Deliverability

Deliverability means getting into the inbox, not the spam folder. ActiveCampaign takes it seriously. Most customers send from shared IP pools that ActiveCampaign's deliverability team manages and keeps warm. High volume senders can move to a dedicated IP, and ActiveCampaign's Help Center is clear that a dedicated IP only helps if you send consistently and keep it healthy.

Every plan includes a custom mail server domain, spam checks and link tracking. ActiveCampaign walks you through DKIM, SPF and DMARC setup, and its reports let you filter out opens inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection, which makes open rates more honest.

ActiveCampaign also promotes a January 2024 independent study by EmailTooltester that ranked it first for deliverability. That is a vendor cited, third party result from 2024, not something we measured, so treat it as one data point. Your list quality, authentication and sending habits matter more than the platform.

Integrations

ActiveCampaign lists more than 1,000 integrations, plus an API and webhooks, Zapier and native Gmail and Outlook connections on every plan. It also now connects to Claude and ChatGPT through MCP on every plan, so you can query and act on your account from an AI assistant.

Some integrations depend on your plan:

  • All plans: Shopify, Square and WooCommerce, plus standard CRM integrations such as Pipedrive, Zoho and Freshsales.
  • Plus and above: Google, Facebook and LinkedIn ads, Calendly, Eventbrite, PayPal, Stripe and Slack.
  • Pro and above: Docusign and premium ecommerce integrations for BigCommerce, Magento and Shopify Plus.
  • Enterprise only: premium CRM integrations for Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics, and Zendesk.

That last point catches people out. If your sales team runs on Salesforce and you want a native sync, you need Enterprise. Pipedrive users can connect on any plan, which makes ActiveCampaign plus Pipedrive a popular, affordable combination.

Other features worth knowing

  • Landing pages and site messages on Plus and above. Forms are on every plan, but Starter is limited to standard and inline forms.
  • Ecommerce tools: abandoned cart on every plan, product catalog blocks and coupons from Plus, and recurring payments from Pro.
  • Reporting: campaign, automation and contact reports on every plan. Revenue, sales and ecommerce reporting from Plus. Conversion tracking and revenue attribution from Pro. Custom reporting is an add on for Plus and Pro and included in Enterprise.
  • SMS and WhatsApp: SMS is an add on for Plus and above. WhatsApp is sold as separate messaging plans you can combine with Plus, Pro or Enterprise.
  • Transactional email: an add on powered by Postmark, available on every plan, for receipts and password resets.
  • Security: single sign on and HIPAA support on Enterprise.

Starter

Starter is ActiveCampaign's entry plan. It replaced the old Lite plan, which started at $29 a month, and it is aimed at individuals getting their first automations running. You get 1 user (with the option to add 1 more), email campaigns, multistep email automation capped at 5 actions per automation, forms, site tracking, basic ecommerce integrations and campaign reporting.

Starter tops out at 25,000 contacts, existing customers don't qualify for it, and you can't add the Enhanced CRM or SMS.

Plus

Plus is where ActiveCampaign becomes a proper automation platform. The 5 action cap disappears, and you get cross channel marketing, landing pages, site messages, generative AI for content and images, ad audience integrations, custom user permissions and revenue reporting. Plus is also the first plan that can buy the Enhanced CRM and SMS add ons.

The catch is seats. Plus still includes just 1 user, so a team of three pays for extra seats.

Pro

Pro is the plan we'd point most growing marketing teams toward. It includes 3 users, predictive sending, conditional content, advanced segmentation, the automations map, automation A/B testing, conversion tracking and revenue attribution, and 200 active business goals. Support moves up to priority email and chat, with priority onboarding and migration help.

Enterprise

Enterprise adds 5 users, single sign on, HIPAA support, premium segmentation, custom objects, custom reporting, unlimited inbox previews, Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics integrations, a dedicated account team and an uptime SLA. It suits regulated industries and larger teams.

Add ons and extra costs

ActiveCampaign's base price is honest, but the add ons are where budgets grow. Here is what costs extra:

  • Enhanced CRM (Pipelines or Sales Engagement): Plus and above. Price shown at checkout.
  • SMS: Plus and above.
  • Custom Reporting: add on for Plus and Pro, included in Enterprise.
  • Transactional Email: any plan.
  • WhatsApp: separate messaging plans.
  • Extra users beyond the included seats.
  • Extra AI credits: packs of 100 from $10 a month.

On the plus side, ActiveCampaign says there are no setup costs or hidden fees, migration is free, and it advertises a full refund if you aren't satisfied in the first 30 days. There is also a 14 day free trial based on the Professional plan, limited to 1 user and 100 email sends.

Who ActiveCampaign is best for

ActiveCampaign is a great fit if you are

  • An ecommerce brand on Shopify or WooCommerce that wants abandoned cart, post purchase and win back flows without paying enterprise prices.
  • A creator, coach or course business that sells through email sequences and needs tagging and branching logic.
  • A B2B company with a light sales process where marketing nurtures leads and a small team closes them.
  • An agency that builds automations for clients and wants a reliable, well known platform.
  • A team leaving Mailchimp because it needs real automation and segmentation.

Who should skip ActiveCampaign

  • Sales led teams with several reps. A dedicated CRM such as Pipedrive or Salesforce fits better.
  • Businesses that want everything on one platform, including service desk and website. HubSpot is the stronger all in one choice.
  • Very small senders on a zero budget. If you send one newsletter a month, a free email tool is enough.
  • Teams with huge, low engagement lists. Contact based pricing will punish you unless you clean the list first.

Frequently asked questions

Is ActiveCampaign worth it in 2026?

For businesses where email and automation drive revenue, yes. ActiveCampaign gives you one of the strongest automation builders on the market at a fraction of what HubSpot Professional costs. It is less of a fit if you need a full sales CRM or a free plan.

How much does ActiveCampaign cost?

ActiveCampaign prices by plan and contact count. At 1,000 contacts billed yearly, ActiveCampaign listed Starter from $15 a month, Plus at $49, Pro at $79 and Enterprise at $145. Prices rise as your list grows, and monthly billing costs more. Pricing checked November 2026.

Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?

No. ActiveCampaign offers a 14 day free trial instead. The trial uses Professional plan features, is limited to 1 user and allows up to 100 email sends. ActiveCampaign also advertises a 30 day money back guarantee on paid plans.

Is ActiveCampaign a CRM?

Partly. Every plan includes contact profiles, custom fields and tags. Pipelines, deal records, lead scoring and win probability come from the Enhanced CRM add ons, which you can only buy on Plus, Pro or Enterprise. It suits marketing led businesses more than sales led teams.

What is Active Intelligence?

Active Intelligence is ActiveCampaign's AI layer. It helps you build campaigns, automations, segments and reports from plain English prompts, and it surfaces weekly insights on your campaigns. Every plan includes 500 AI credits a month for advanced actions such as image generation and translated campaigns.

Is ActiveCampaign better than HubSpot?

For email marketing and automation on a budget, usually yes. ActiveCampaign Pro costs far less than HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional and has no onboarding fee. HubSpot is better if you want marketing, sales and service on one platform with a free CRM to start.

How many users does ActiveCampaign include?

Starter and Plus include 1 user seat, Pro includes 3 and Enterprise includes 5. Starter can add 1 extra seat. Plus, Pro and Enterprise can add more seats at extra cost.

Can I cancel ActiveCampaign at any time?

Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing date. Annual plans are paid for the year. ActiveCampaign advertises a full refund if you are not satisfied within 30 days, so test it properly in that window and check the terms at checkout.

Final verdict

ActiveCampaign earns an 8.5 out of 10. It is the tool we recommend when email and automation are how you make money, and you don't want to pay HubSpot prices to do it well.

The automation builder is the star: deep, visual and on every plan. Segmentation is strong, the AI is useful, and there is no onboarding fee.

The weak spots are clear too. Starter is tight, the CRM is an add on, Plus has just one seat, and contact based pricing grows with your list. If you need a real sales CRM, pair ActiveCampaign with one, or look at HubSpot instead.

Our advice: start the 14 day trial, which runs on Pro features. Build one real automation, such as your welcome series or abandoned cart flow. If it works the way you want, Plus is the sensible starting point for most businesses, and Pro is worth it once you have a team of three or want predictive sending and attribution.
