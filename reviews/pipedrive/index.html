What is Pipedrive?

Pipedrive is a sales CRM. That sounds obvious, but it matters. Most big CRMs try to be everything: marketing, sales, service, websites and data. Pipedrive starts from one question. What should this salesperson do next to move this deal forward?

Everything in the product is shaped around that idea. Your deals sit as cards on a board. Each column is a stage in your sales process. You drag a deal to the next stage when it moves, and Pipedrive nudges you to schedule the next activity so nothing goes cold. Pipedrive says it is trusted by more than 100,000 sales teams around the world.

On top of the core CRM, Pipedrive sells four plans: Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. These replaced the older Essential, Advanced, Professional, Power and Enterprise plans in 2025. Pipedrive's own help center maps them like this: Essential became Lite, Advanced became Growth, Professional and Power merged into Premium, and Enterprise became Ultimate. If you read an older review that mentions Essential or Power, the details are out of date.

Around the plans sit optional add-ons for lead generation, email campaigns, project delivery, documents and website visitor tracking. There is also a growing set of AI tools, led by Pipedrive Nova for meeting intelligence and the Sales Assistant for questions about your data.

The simple version: Pipedrive is a focused pipeline tool that you can extend when you need to. You pay for what you use, and you never wade through menus built for a marketing team you don't have.

Key features

Below we cover the features that matter most to buyers, what each plan includes and where the limits sit. Plan details come from Pipedrive's official pricing page and help center, checked November 2026.

Pipeline view

The pipeline view is why people choose Pipedrive. It is a visual board of your deals, grouped by stage, with the value of each stage added up at the top. You can see at a glance where money is stuck and which deals need attention.

What we like most is how it pushes action. Pipedrive reminds you when deals have no activity scheduled, so reps always know what to do next. A deal rotting setting highlights deals that have sat too long in a stage. You can run separate pipelines for different products or teams, and on every plan you can customize deal cards to show the fields you care about.

The same workspace holds leads, deals, contacts, calendar events and activities. Leads land in a Leads Inbox first, where you can label them hot, warm or cold before they enter the pipeline. That keeps the pipeline clean and reserved for real opportunities.

  • Drag and drop deal stages with value totals per stage
  • Multiple pipelines and customizable deal cards
  • Activity reminders and deal rotting alerts
  • Leads Inbox with hot, warm and cold labels, plus automatic lead routing on Premium
  • Forecast view and forecast reports from the Growth plan
  • Subscriptions and installment tracking for recurring revenue from Growth

There is one limit to understand. Pipedrive caps the combined number of active leads and deals per company. Lite allows 2,500 per seat, Growth 5,000, Premium 15,000 and Ultimate 20,000, up to a maximum of 300,000 per company. Contacts have no limit. For most small teams this never bites, but high volume inbound teams should check it before buying.

Automations and sequences

Automations are where Pipedrive goes from a tidy board to a real time saver. They start on the Growth plan. Lite has none, which is the biggest reason we steer most teams past Lite.

Each automation is a simple if then rule. A trigger, such as a deal moving to a stage or a date approaching, starts the automation. Conditions decide whether it runs. Actions do the work: create an activity, update a field, send an email from your synced inbox, post to Slack or Microsoft Teams, create a project, or fire a webhook. You can add delays, "wait for" conditions and if/else branches to split one automation into several paths.

The builder is easy to read, which is the point. A sales manager can set up "when a deal reaches Proposal Sent, create a follow up call in three days and notify the owner" without help from an admin.

Sequences are the other half of sales automation. They let you build a series of personalized emails and follow up tasks that nurture a deal over time. They arrive on Growth, alongside full Gmail and Outlook sync with open and click tracking. For outbound teams, Growth is really the starting line.

AI Sales Assistant and Pipedrive Nova

Pipedrive's AI is practical rather than flashy. It sits on your CRM data and focuses on saving reps time. There are three parts worth knowing.

Sales Assistant lives at the top of the app, next to search. You can ask it questions in text or by voice, such as "which deals need my attention?" or "what is forecasted for next quarter?", and it answers from your Pipedrive data. It can also summarize content, draft notes and emails, and walk you through how a feature works using Pipedrive's knowledge base. Answers show their sources, which helps you check them. One caveat: Pipedrive's help center labels Sales Assistant as a beta feature, and an admin has to accept the AI beta terms before the team can use it.

Pipedrive Nova is built in meeting intelligence. Before a meeting it writes a brief from deal context, contact history and recent activity. During the meeting, an AI notetaker joins Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams to transcribe, or a desktop companion app for macOS and Windows captures audio without a bot joining. Afterward it produces a summary, follow up actions and suggested CRM updates. Nothing is saved until you approve it. Pipedrive says Nova is included on all plans at no extra cost.

Plan based AI tools round things out. Every plan gets an AI import assistant, AI report creation in Insights, AI powered Marketplace search and app recommendations. Premium and Ultimate add AI email writing, AI notifications that predict deal win probability and recommend next steps, plus AI email summaries and suggested replies, which are in beta.

Pipedrive says it does not let third parties use customer data to train AI models, and does not use your data to train its own models without permission. That matters if you handle sensitive deal information.

Our take: Nova is the standout because it attacks the most hated part of selling, updating the CRM after calls. The Sales Assistant is useful but still in beta, so treat it as a helper, not a reason to buy.

Add-ons follow your plan's billing cycle. Pipedrive's pricing page shows "starting from" figures, and the final price depends on volume, so confirm on the Pipedrive pricing page.

LeadBooster bundles four lead generation tools. The Chatbot greets website visitors, asks qualifying questions and creates leads or deals from the answers. Live Chat lets a rep take over the conversation from the web or mobile app. Web Forms capture sign ups straight into Pipedrive. Prospector is an outbound database that Pipedrive says covers over 400 million profiles and 10 million companies, and LeadBooster includes 10 Prospector credits a month. If inbound leads matter to you, LeadBooster is a big part of why Premium is good value.

Campaigns is Pipedrive's email marketing tool. You get a drag and drop builder, templates, segmentation using your CRM data, scheduling, double opt in, sender domain authentication and real time engagement reports. There are two tiers, Standard and Premium, and you choose a subscriber count between 1,000 and 100,000. The more subscribers you buy, the less you pay per subscriber. A 21 day trial lets you add up to 500 subscribers, and Pipedrive requires an account review before you can buy. It works well for newsletters and simple nurture emails. It is not a replacement for a full email marketing platform.

Projects picks up where the sale ends. When a deal closes, you can create a project with an AI assisted brief built from the deal's emails, notes and files. You get tasks, dependencies, Gantt timelines (in beta), automations and reporting, with mobile access on Android now and iOS listed as coming soon. Agencies and service businesses that hand work from sales to delivery will like this most.

Smart Docs lets you create quotes, proposals and contracts from templates that fill in deal, contact and product data automatically. You share them as trackable links, get notified when they are opened, and request legally binding eSignatures. It connects to Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive and SharePoint for storage. The add-on comes with a 14 day trial that includes five signature requests.

Mobile app

Pipedrive's iOS and Android apps are some of the best in the category for field sales. They are not a cut down viewer. You get the pipeline view, activities, calendar, contacts, filtering, deal and contact details, and push notifications.

The features that matter on the road are well covered. A Nearby view shows contacts and deals around you. A Focus view highlights what to do today. You can call and text from the app, see caller ID, record audio notes, upload photos and files, and work offline. On Android you also get a business card scanner and full call logging. On iOS, Pipedrive logs only outgoing calls made in the app. Since 2025, every plan also supports web to mobile calls: click a number in the web app and finish the call on your phone, with the call logged automatically.

The apps need Android 11 or iOS 18 or later. If your reps spend more time in cars than at desks, the mobile app alone is a strong reason to shortlist Pipedrive.

Integrations, reporting and security

Pipedrive connects to 500+ apps through its Marketplace, including Zapier, Zoom and Lemlist. Inside automations you get native actions for Slack, Microsoft Teams, Trello and Asana, plus webhooks for anything else. API access scales with your plan, from 30,000 tokens per seat on Lite to 210,000 per seat on Ultimate.

Insights is the reporting tool. You can build reports on deals, activities, revenue and forecasts, or type a request in plain English and let AI build the report. Premium adds custom scoring, company data enrichment, formula fields and group level permissions. Ultimate adds account security alerts, password rules, IP and time based access restrictions, contact data enrichment and a sandbox account for testing changes safely.

Lite

Lite gives you the visual pipeline, leads, deals, contacts, calendar, AI report creation, data import and the 500+ integrations. Nova meeting intelligence is included too. What you don't get is automation, sequences or full email sync. Lite is fine for one person keeping deals organized. For a team, it feels limited fast.

Growth

Growth is the plan we recommend for most small sales teams. It adds full Gmail, Outlook or company email sync, bulk email with open and click tracking, automations, sequences, subscriptions, forecast reports, a meeting scheduler and the contacts timeline. At $39 per seat per month on annual billing, it is the point where Pipedrive starts paying for itself in saved admin time.

Premium

Premium is Pipedrive's most popular plan, and for teams with inbound leads it is often the best value. It includes LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs, which would otherwise be add-ons. It also adds custom scoring, company data enrichment, AI email writing and summaries, shared team inboxes, contracts with eSignatures, formula fields, flexible permissions and phone support for admins. If you would buy LeadBooster and Smart Docs on Growth anyway, do the math. Premium may cost less.

Ultimate

Ultimate is for teams that need control. It adds security alerts, password requirements, IP and time based access rules, contact data enrichment, a sandbox account and longer phone support hours (Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 11:00 pm CET). It also raises every limit and includes discounts on PandaDoc, CloudTalk and Surfe subscriptions. Most small teams will never need it.

Hidden costs to plan for

Pipedrive is transparent, but the real price is not always the headline price. Watch for these:

  • Add-ons. Campaigns and Web Visitors cost extra on every plan. LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs cost extra on Lite and Growth.
  • Seats, not users. Pipedrive charges per seat whether or not it is assigned to a user. Remove unused seats at renewal.
  • Top ups. If you hit limits on automations, reports, leads and deals or API tokens, you can buy top ups instead of upgrading, but they add to the bill.
  • Monthly billing. The headline prices assume annual billing.
  • VAT. Prices exclude VAT, which EU customers pay unless they provide a valid VAT number.

The good news: there is no mandatory onboarding fee, and Pipedrive says implementation is free on plans that cost over $400 a year.

Who Pipedrive is best for

Pipedrive is a great fit if you are

  • A small B2B sales team of 2 to 25 reps that wants pipeline visibility without a big setup project.
  • A founder doing sales who needs to stop losing follow ups in an inbox or spreadsheet.
  • An agency or service business that sells, sends proposals and then delivers projects, especially on Premium.
  • A field sales team that needs a strong mobile app with offline mode and calling.
  • A sales manager without an admin who wants to build automations and reports personally.

Use cases where Pipedrive shines

  • Outbound prospecting. Prospector finds leads, sequences follow up and Nova handles call notes.
  • Inbound lead capture. Chatbot and Web Forms create leads, and routing rules assign them to the right rep fast.
  • Quote to close. Smart Docs sends trackable proposals with eSignatures straight from the deal.
  • Sale to delivery handoff. Projects turns won deals into tasks without retyping anything.

Who should skip Pipedrive

  • Marketing led businesses. If campaigns, landing pages and lead scoring drive growth, HubSpot or ActiveCampaign fit better. See our best marketing automation software guide.
  • Teams that need a free CRM. HubSpot's free tools or Zoho CRM's free edition cost nothing to start.
  • Large or complex sales organizations. Multiple business units, territories and approvals call for Salesforce.
  • Support heavy businesses. Pipedrive has no full help desk. Look at a CRM with service tools built in.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pipedrive worth it in 2026?

For sales led teams that want a clean pipeline and fast follow ups, yes. Growth at $39 per seat per month billed annually is the sweet spot for most small teams because it unlocks automations, sequences and full email sync. If you need serious marketing automation or a free plan, look at HubSpot or ActiveCampaign instead.

How much does Pipedrive cost?

Pipedrive has four plans billed per seat. On annual billing they cost US$168 (Lite), US$468 (Growth), US$708 (Premium) and US$948 (Ultimate) per seat per year, which works out to $14, $39, $59 and $79 per seat per month. Monthly billing costs more. Prices exclude VAT. Pricing checked November 2026.

Does Pipedrive have a free plan?

No. Pipedrive does not offer a free plan. It offers a free 14 day trial with full access and no credit card required, and you can invite teammates to the trial.

What add-ons does Pipedrive sell?

Pipedrive lists five add-ons: LeadBooster (from $32.50), Projects (from $16), Campaigns (from $13.33), Web Visitors (from $41) and Smart Docs (from $32.50). LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs are included on Premium and Ultimate. Campaigns and Web Visitors are paid extras on every plan.

Does Pipedrive have AI features?

Yes. Every plan includes Pipedrive Nova for meeting briefs, transcripts and suggested CRM updates, plus AI report creation, an AI import assistant and the Sales Assistant, which is in beta. AI email writing, AI email summaries, suggested replies and AI notifications are on Premium and Ultimate.

Is Pipedrive good for email marketing?

Only for light email marketing. Campaigns by Pipedrive is a paid add-on that gives you a drag and drop builder, segmentation, scheduling and reports, priced by subscriber count. If email is your main revenue channel, a dedicated platform like ActiveCampaign will do more.

Is Pipedrive better than HubSpot?

Pipedrive is better if you only need a sales pipeline and want transparent per seat pricing. HubSpot is better if marketing, sales and service need to share one platform, or if you want a free CRM to start. Our HubSpot review and CRM buying guide cover the trade offs.

What support does Pipedrive offer?

Pipedrive says all users get 24/7 live chat support and email support. Phone support is available to admins on the Premium and Ultimate plans, with longer phone hours on Ultimate.

Final verdict

Pipedrive earns an 8.7 out of 10 because it does one job extremely well. It helps salespeople see their deals, take the next step and spend less time on admin. Setup is fast, the mobile app is excellent, Nova takes care of call notes, and the pricing is honest and published.

It is not for everyone. There is no free plan, Lite is too thin for a team, and marketing tools are a paid extra. Larger organizations with complex processes will outgrow it.

Our advice is simple. Start the 14 day trial on Growth. Put a week of real deals into it. If you capture inbound leads or send proposals, price Premium too, because the included add-ons often make it the better deal.
